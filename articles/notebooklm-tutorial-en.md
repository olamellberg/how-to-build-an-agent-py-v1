# NotebookLM in the AI Playbook
**Version 1.0** | 2026-02-04
### One notebook per article — with a podcast and a quiz

NotebookLM is a companion to each AI Playbook article. For **every article**, there is an existing notebook you use to:

- recap quickly (podcast)
- verify understanding (quiz)
- access sources + team-specific takeaways (sources/notes)

Example (NotebookLM): <a href="https://notebooklm.google.com/notebook/b947a18d-cd83-4c71-ac70-4eaa0fd555c7?pli=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open example notebook</a>

---

## Purpose: from reading to applying

An article helps you understand. The notebook is designed to help you **apply** the content in 10–20 minutes:

- the podcast gives you the big picture fast
- the quiz reveals gaps and common pitfalls
- the notes show how we tend to use the ideas in the team

---

## How to use the notebook (recommended flow)

- **1) Open the notebook for the article** (from the article link or your NotebookLM list).
- **2) Listen to the podcast** first to get the overview.
- **3) Take the quiz** without cheating. Note 1–2 questions you missed.
- **4) Re-read the relevant parts** of the article that the quiz pointed to.
- **5) Skim the notes** and pick one thing to try in your next work session.
